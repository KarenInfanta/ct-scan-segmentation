01 / The challenge

From raw pixels to meaningful regions.

Segmentation helps isolate regions of interest in medical images. This project compares the reported performance of AOA against four alternative optimization algorithms.

01 — OBJECTIVE

Compare algorithms

Evaluate AOA alongside CLO, DBO, DMO, and EFO in the reported CT image segmentation comparisons.

02 — APPROACH

Threshold-based analysis

Study multilevel thresholding as the segmentation approach and examine the reported algorithm results.

03 — OUTCOME

Make results readable

Bring the reported accuracy figures into one clear visual comparison for easier interpretation.
